How it works

Four taps from stranger to connection.

A room full of builders, and you know none of their names. HAUNT fixes the first ten seconds of every conversation.

01

Sign up in a minute

Scan the QR. Name, GitHub, a costume, three selfies, tick consent. Or sign in with LinkedIn and your photo is the selfie.

02

Point your phone

The camera finds them, paints the costume they picked onto their face, and opens their card. Tap Haunt.

03

Their phone shrieks

Sound, a flash and a buzz, with who found them. One tap on Haunt back and you're even.

04

Gone in one tap

Had enough? Turn Findable off and every phone in the room stops naming you, at once.

Costume lenses

Your costume, drawn on your face. On their phone.

Pick Vampire, Witch, Demon, Ghost, Skeleton or Pumpkin. When someone finds you, their phone tracks your face at camera rate and paints it: glowing irises that blink with you, fangs that show when you open your mouth, 3D bats circling your head.

  • 468-point face mesh, MediaPipe + WebGL
  • Runs on the hunter's phone, never in a cloud
  • Only people who opted in can wear one
The big screen

The room plays along.

Put /screen on the venue TV: a live feed of who haunted whom, most haunted, top hunters, and one QR code to join. Every haunt lands on it the moment it happens.

Afterwards

Leave with everyone you met.

Your card keeps tonight's connections: who you found and who found you, with their GitHub and LinkedIn. One tap copies the list. And a matchmaking agent, the Scout, sends you clues about people you should meet, never their names.

  • Sign in with GitHub, LinkedIn, Google or Apple
  • Snap mode for Apple Vision Pro
  • Organizer tools: admin, bulk enroll, calibration
Privacy first

Nobody has to trust it.

A face-recognition app at a party only works if the rules are enforced in code, not in a policy nobody reads.

📱

Matching runs on the phone

Detector, landmarks, an aligned crop and a 7 MB open-source embedder in WebAssembly. Video never leaves the device.

✋

Opt-in, enforced by the server

Phones only ever receive the signatures of people who ticked consent. It's a server-side filter, not a UI checkbox.

❔

Strangers stay strangers

A face that isn't registered shows as "unknown". Never named, never stored.

🔌

A kill switch that works

Turn Findable off and you vanish from every open phone instantly, pushed over a WebSocket.

🚫

No face goes to an AI model

No image or face signature is ever sent to a hosted model. The agents work from events and profiles only.

🗑️

Built to be deleted

The server keeps 512-number signatures, cards and events in one file. Delete it after the event.

Open source

Run your own haunted house.

One Node process, plain ES modules, no build step. Deploys to Fly.io in one command for about $2 a month.

$ git clone https://github.com/mthomas100/haunt && cd haunt
$ npm install
$ npm start        # http://localhost:3000
$ npm run tunnel   # phones need HTTPS for the camera
$ npm test         # API, pushes, consent filter, kill switch
